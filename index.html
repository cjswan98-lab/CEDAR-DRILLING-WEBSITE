Cross-border trucking

Alongside construction, Cedar Drilling coordinates road haulage for Makomo Resources, moving coal and metallurgical coke from the Hwange coalfield in Zimbabwe to industrial and power customers across the region. Loads are staged at the mine, cleared at the border and delivered to site on a scheduled cycle, so offtakers receive consistent tonnage without managing the corridor themselves.

Hwange to South Africa

Hwange · Bulawayo · Beitbridge · Limpopo / Gauteng

Metallurgical coke to smelters and steelworks. Beitbridge clearing and convoy scheduling run to fixed weekly slots.

Hwange to Zambia

Hwange · Victoria Falls · Livingstone · Copperbelt

Thermal coal to power stations and process plants. Bridge crossing and Zambian transit documentation handled end to end.

Hwange to D.R. Congo

Hwange · Kasumbalesa · Lubumbashi / Kolwezi

Thermal coal for power generation and mining operations in Haut-Katanga and Lualaba, with in-transit tracking to delivery.

Fleet coordination

Contracted side-tipper and flat-deck fleets sized to the offtake schedule, with loading at the mine siding and turnaround targets per corridor.

Border and compliance

Pre-lodged customs entries, MMCZ export documentation, weighbridge and certificate control at each crossing.

Delivery assurance

Sealed loads, per-truck weighbridge reconciliation at destination and monthly tonnage reporting to the customer.

Road to rail

Corridors are planned so that volumes can migrate to rail as capacity opens, with trucking retained for first- and last-mile legs.

Oya Wind Farm – 128 MW

Wind | Civils
South Africa

Main civils contractor for the 128 MW Oya Wind Farm: internal access roads from the R356 intersection to WTG 17, including bulk earthworks, roadbed preparation, layer works, compaction and gravel surfacing. Design and construction of low-water crossings and drainage structures for all-weather access, with site establishment, environmental management and QA. The roads provide turbine delivery access, long-term maintenance routes and integrated erosion and stormwater control.
